## Deposit facts used
- Host is yttrofluorite (Y-fluorite), not ionic clay, not Mountain Pass bastnäsite.
- Dilute sulfuric heap leach is the mineralogy-native liberation path (Pingitore; US 9,890,441). Cited Dy extraction 57.5% on a 1/2–1 in crush in that patent’s column language; many bucket tests lower.
- Separation problem is Dy vs Y (and Tb) in an HREE-rich liquor. PC88A / D2EHPA literature is from monazite or Y-concentrate systems, **not** Round Top PLS — flag that whenever quoting 97% Dy2O3.
- USAR Jan 2025: 99.1% Dy2O3 from Round Top ore at Wheat Ridge. Existence proof, flowsheet closed.
