# 2026-09-14 — membranes / IL as a Dy/Y stage-count cutter

Focus: whether a supported liquid membrane or ionic-liquid carrier can cut mixer-settler count on a Y-heavy sulfate liquor. Not a reprint of yesterday’s PC88A stage-count page or the F/Th/U cut.

## Fit to this host (not clay, not bastnäsite)
A membrane does not dissolve yttrofluorite. It sits after the dilute-H2SO4 heap and after an F/U/Th cut. The liquor is Y >> Dy in sulfate with residual fluoride. Literature SLMs win on contacting area (extract and strip in one module) and sometimes on Y-preference with carboxylate ILs — the opposite of a Dy-first magnet spec unless you take Y out the membrane and leave Dy in the raffinate. Fluoride and gypsum are untested poisons for IL-impregnated pores. Emulsion membranes are a lab toy on 100 mg/L Dy; they are not a heap-PLS plant.
