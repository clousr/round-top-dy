## Fit to this host (not clay, not bastnäsite)
Yttrofluorite plus accessory cryolite / gearksutite / ralstonite puts F into sulfate liquor. Neutralizing hard enough to drop gypsum also drops free acid and can occlude LREE/HREE. Recycle is therefore an F-cut problem, not a pump problem. The 90–95% inert gangue is why 22–26 kg/t is even plausible; a limestone host would eat more acid than the Dy is worth.
