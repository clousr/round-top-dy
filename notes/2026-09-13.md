# 2026-09-13 — PLS fluoride, Th, U after sulfate heap

Focus: what the dilute-H2SO4 heap actually puts into solution besides Dy, and how that liquor has to be cleaned before SX. Not a reprint of the 2026-09-12 scaffold.

## What was read
- Pingitore et al., J. Rare Earths 32 (2014) 90–96 (open PDF via TMRC): yttrofluorite host; F listed with Be, Li, U, Th, Nb, Ta as co-enriched elements; dilute ambient H2SO4 heap is the lithologic path. https://tmrcorp.com/_resources/reports/2014_Jan_Journal_RE_32.pdf
- US 9,890,441 B2 / US20160138133A1: heap leach of yttrofluorite with H2SO4 or HCl; cited column/bucket recoveries already in methods.json. Patent also states U and Th can be partially stripped in a first-stage contact with little REE loss, then more U/Th in a second stage. https://patents.google.com/patent/US9890441B2/en
- AGU 2014 V23D-4827: dilute H2SO4 “also releases some of the Be, Li, U, F, and Th from other soluble minor minerals.” https://agu.confex.com/agu/fm14/webprogram/Paper19765.html
- Hazen QEMSCAN in the 2014 PEA appendix: yttrium-rich fluorite is the main Y+Dy carrier; ABWL residue Y extraction ~94% cited; other F minerals in the rhyolite include cryolite, gearksutite, ralstonite besides fluorite. https://tmrcorp.com/_resources/reports/Amended_TRER_NI43-101_PEA_FINAL_28April2014.pdf
- USAR Nov 2025 investor deck (public PDF): “up to 70% REE extraction” in column work; SX pilot “validated separation of U, Th, Hf and Zr, HREEs.” Flowsheet internals still closed. https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6778240fbac05d2a404af1c0/6913911c62a7c7d74ad9928a_USAR%20-%20Investor%20Presentation_Nov2025.pdf
- Analog (not Round Top PLS): Al3+ complexation to keep F in solution and cut HF off-gas during sulfate leach of F-bearing REE ore (CN117025955B). Hot, concentrated, bastnäsite-class — not a heap analog. https://patents.google.com/patent/CN117025955B/en

## What changed in the comparison
Added method row `pls-f-th-u` (PLS cleanup: F / Th / U) because the heap is not a separator and the liquor is not a clean Dy sulfate. Updated heap-leach waste line to name F minerals + radionuclides as first-order PLS impurities.

- Dy 57.5% / Y 61.1% on 1/2–1 in crush; many buckets 42–49% Y+HREE — **cited** US 9,890,441.
- Lab leach “up to 90% YHREE” at ~1/2 in crush (UTEP thesis / related Pingitore porosity paper) — **cited** as an upper bench figure, not a heap design number. https://scholarworks.utep.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=3880&context=open_etd
- Hazen ABWL Y extraction ~94% — **cited** PEA appendix; that is acid-bake + water leach, **not** ambient heap.
- USAR “up to 70% REE extraction” columns — **cited** company slide, element split and acid dose not given.
- F concentration in Round Top PLS, HF off-gas rate at ambient dilute H2SO4, kg acid / t ore on a lined heap, $/kg Dy after F+U+Th cut — **unknown**. Experiment: instrument F, U, Th, Al, Ca, SO4 on a closed-circuit column PLS at 5–20 g/L H2SO4, 1/2-in crush, then measure Dy loss across a two-contact U/Th cut and a CaF2 or Al-F sink.
- Al-complex / cryolite / lime-gypsum F sinks — **order-of-magnitude process options** from other F+sulfate industries; recoveries on this rhyolite **unknown**.

## Fit to this host (not clay, not bastnäsite)
Dissolving yttrofluorite in H2SO4 necessarily releases F with Y+HREE. Accessory cryolite / gearksutite / ralstonite and a Ca-Th-Pb fluoride can add more F and Th. Ambient dilute acid should limit HF vapor versus bastnäsite bake, but F still reports to PLS and will fight gypsum precipitation, solvent stability, and discharge limits. U/Th must be cut before a Dy/Y SX train; the patent claims a two-stage contact can do that with low REE loss — **cited as a claim**, not as a measured Round Top plant balance.

# 2026-09-13 increment — PC88A vs D2EHPA stage count on a Y-heavy liquor

Focus: how many mixer-settler contacts the Dy/Y cut actually takes, and why a Round Top sulfate PLS is a worse feed than Thakur’s monazite Y-concentrate. Not a reprint of the morning F/Th/U page.

## What was read
- Thakur, Solvent Extr. Ion Exch. 18 (2000) 853–875 (abstract / secondary open summaries): Dy/Y cut from a monazite-derived Y-concentrate (~60% Y2O3) with D2EHPA and PC88A (EHEHPA). Mini mixer-settlers: Dy2O3 ~97% purity at ~93% Dy recovery; Y2O3 ~93% purity at ~95% Y recovery. Low adjacent-pair β is the stated reason a cascade is required. https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/07366290008934711
- Mishra et al., Hydrometallurgy 56 (2000) 33–40: later PC88A cycle on a Dy-rich cut from the same monazite train (not Round Top). https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304386X00000645
- Mohammadi, Forsberg, et al., Hydrometallurgy 156 (2015) 215–224: Nd/Dy/Y from HCl with D2EHPA, EHEHPA, and mixtures. Mix better for Y/Dy at 0.06–0.09 M total extractant; pure EHEHPA better at 0.15 M. Chloride synthetic, not sulfate heap PLS. https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:kth:diva-174945
- Krishnamurthy / Gupta review (open NML PDF): D2EHPA stronger extractant, harsher strip; PC88A higher β and strips at lower acid; monazite chloride trains use “a large number of stages” because adjacent β is small. https://eprints.nmlindia.org/9390/1/63..1-s2.0-S0304386X16300603-main.pdf
- INL/OSTI mid–HREE mixer-settler comparison of PC88A vs Cyanex 572: a 29–30 stage circuit (extract + scrub + strip) was the tested unit; 10 extract stages pulled Ho+Y, 15 scrub stages still left Gd bleed on PC88A. Not Dy/Y-only and not Round Top. https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1363891
- Ahn et al., J. Korean Inst. Resour. Recycl. (open PDF): PC88A on mixed REE including Y and Dy; β(Dy/Y) tabulated ~3.53 at eq. pH 0.5, A/O 1, 25 °C — **cited analog**, chloride/nitrate-style synthetic mix, not Round Top PLS. https://www.koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201725650932404.pdf
- USAR public filings / decks 2025–2026: Wheat Ridge demo planned with five SX circuits and “>500 mixers/settlers”; circuits named for HREE including Dy, Tb, Y plus Hf/Zr. Extractant identity and stage map unpublished. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1970622/000121390026047162/ea0287130-03.pdf
- USAR Nov 2025 deck (already logged): SX1/SX2 piloted; U/Th/Hf/Zr/HREE split claimed; no PC88A/D2EHPA name. https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6778240fbac05d2a404af1c0/6913911c62a7c7d74ad9928a_USAR%20-%20Investor%20Presentation_Nov2025.pdf

## What changed in the comparison
Patched `sx-pc88a` and `sx-d2ehpa` recovery / energy / scale lines: the workhorse numbers are monazite-Y or synthetic-chloride, β(Dy/Y) on organophosphorus acids is typically a few units, and a mountain-scale Y-heavy sulfate PLS will need a multi-bank cascade — USAR’s public “five circuits / >500 mixer-settlers” is an existence envelope, not a recipe.

## Numbers (tagged)
- Thakur 2000 Dy ~97% oxide / ~93% recovery and Y ~93% oxide / ~95% recovery — **cited** on monazite Y-concentrate in chloride-class SX, **not** Round Top heap PLS.
- Ahn 2017 β(Dy/Y) ≈ 3.53 (PC88A, eq. pH 0.5, A/O 1) — **cited analog**.
- INL 30-stage mid/HREE PC88A vs Cyanex 572 mixer-settler — **cited** as equipment scale for adjacent HREE, not a Dy/Y plant balance for this rhyolite.
- USAR five SX circuits and >500 mixer-settlers at Wheat Ridge — **cited** company/SEC language; extractant, O/A, acidity, and Dy recovery across those stages — **unknown**.
- Mixer-settler count and acid inventory to take cleaned Round Top sulfate PLS to magnet-spec Dy2O3 — **unknown**. Experiment: McCabe–Thiele on F-cut, U/Th-cut sulfate PLS at heap tenor for Dy, Y, Tb, Er with 0.2–1.0 M PC88A and D2EHPA in kerosene, then a 8–20 stage mini-cascade mass balance.
- $/kg Dy for the SX bank — **unknown**. Reagent order-of-magnitude: PC88A inventory + saponification alkali or mineral-acid strip + kerosene makeup; D2EHPA shifts cost from extractant inventory toward strip acid.

## Fit to this host (not clay, not bastnäsite)
Round Top yttrofluorite puts **Y >> Dy** into a dilute sulfate liquor with residual F after the heap. Thakur’s feed was already a 60% Y2O3 monazite fraction in a chloride SX culture. Adjacent β of ~2–4 means Dy does not jump away from Y in one mixer; Y load will occupy most of the organic capacity. D2EHPA will load HREE harder and cost more acid on strip; PC88A is the usual Dy/Y polish because it strips softer. A D2EHPA + PC88A mix can raise Y/Dy β at low molarity (Mohammadi 2015) — still a chloride synthetic. Sulfate + fluoride will change both D and phase disengagement; that is unmeasured on this PLS.
