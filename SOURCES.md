- Pingitore et al., J. Rare Earths — Y-fluorite host, dilute H2SO4 heap-leach rationale. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1002072114600375
- Open PDF of same: https://tmrcorp.com/_resources/reports/2014_Jan_Journal_RE_32.pdf
- US 9,890,441 B2 — Extraction and recovery of yttrium and rare earth elements (dilute H2SO4 heap leach of Y-fluorite; column tests, Dy extraction cited; two-stage U/Th contact claim). https://patents.google.com/patent/US9890441B2/en
- TRER / Gustavson 2012 / amended 2014 NI 43-101 PEA — resource table, Dy ~31.8 g/t measured; Hazen QEMSCAN appendix (yttrofluorite main Y+Dy carrier; ABWL Y ~94%; F minerals include cryolite, gearksutite, ralstonite). https://tmrcorp.com/_resources/reports/Amended_TRER_NI43-101_PEA_FINAL_28April2014.pdf
- AGU 2014 V23D-4827 — heap also releases Be, Li, U, F, Th. https://agu.confex.com/agu/fm14/webprogram/Paper19765.html
- AGU 2015 V23B-3130 — EPMA maps, yttrofluorite grains.
- UTEP open thesis / related porosity paper — up to 90% YHREE in lab leach at 1/2-in crush. https://scholarworks.utep.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=3880&context=open_etd
- MINING.com 2025-01-28 — USAR 99.1% Dy2O3 sample from Round Top ore, Wheat Ridge CO. https://www.mining.com/usa-rare-earth-produces-dysprosium-oxide-at-texas-round-top-mine/
- USAR Nov 2025 investor presentation — up to 70% REE column extraction; SX pilot U/Th/Hf/Zr/HREE split (no flowsheet recipe). https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6778240fbac05d2a404af1c0/6913911c62a7c7d74ad9928a_USAR%20-%20Investor%20Presentation_Nov2025.pdf

- Batchu et al. 2020 J. Mol. Liq. — PC88A vs Cyanex 572 for Dy(III).
- Padhan & Sarangi / related organophosphorus SX reviews — D2EHPA > PC88A > Cyanex 272 extractant strength.
- CN117025955B — Al2(SO4)3 + H2SO4 leach analog: Al complexes F, later cryolite (not Round Top heap conditions). https://patents.google.com/patent/CN117025955B/en
