How the P/L is built

Contained Dy metal = tonnes processed × grade g/t ÷ 1000. Convert to Dy2O3 with 1.148. Multiply heap recovery (cited column 57.5%) and split recovery (estimate). Revenue = kg × price. Only the Dy share of 2019 PEA opex (~13.5%) is charged so a Dy-only page does not swallow the whole mine. Cash grants ($277M CHIPS + $14.2M Texas + $19.3M DOE CMI) offset capex. The $1.3B CHIPS loan and $1.5B PIPE are financing, not income.
